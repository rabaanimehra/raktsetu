Our vision

Making anemia screening as simple as taking a photograph.

Anemia quietly affects hundreds of millions of people, yet the standard way to detect it — a blood draw and a lab report — is out of reach in many homes, villages and primary health centres. Screening should not depend on how far you live from a laboratory.

RaktSetu — “blood bridge” — is built to connect frontline health workers and the communities they serve with a fast, painless first check that tells them who needs further testing and care.

The gap

Why screening still falls short

Conventional testing works — but it is hard to deliver at the scale and speed communities need.

Invasive by design

Finger-prick and venous blood tests need needles, consumables and trained staff — and put many people, especially children, off getting tested.

Far from the lab

Rural and low-resource settings often lack nearby labs. Samples travel, results are delayed, and follow-up is lost along the way.

Late detection

Symptoms like fatigue and pallor are easy to dismiss. Without routine screening, anemia is often found only after it has become severe.

What is RaktSetu?

A pocket-sized screening tool that reads the colour of the nail bed.

Pallor of the nail bed is a long-recognised clinical sign of anemia. RaktSetu captures a standardised image of the nail bed under controlled lighting and uses a trained AI model to estimate the likelihood of anemia — instantly, on the device.

It is small, portable and designed for low-resource settings, so a health worker can screen a whole community in a single visit.

How it works

Four steps. Under a minute.

No blood, no reagents, no specialist training.

Place the finger

The person rests a finger in the device's imaging chamber, which blocks ambient light.

Capture the nail bed

Controlled, consistent illumination and a calibrated camera photograph the nail bed.

AI analysis

The on-device model analyses colour and texture features linked to haemoglobin levels.

Instant result

A clear screening outcome — likely normal or refer for a blood test — is shown on the spot.

Please note: RaktSetu is a screening aid, not a diagnostic device. It is intended to help identify people who should get a confirmatory blood test. It does not replace clinical evaluation.
Technology

Engineered for the field, not just the lab.

Every design decision balances accuracy, cost and ease of use.

Controlled imaging

A light-shielded chamber with fixed illumination removes the biggest source of error in colour-based screening: changing ambient light.

On-device AI

A lightweight deep learning model runs locally, so results appear in seconds — even without an internet connection.

Privacy first

Images are processed on the device. Patient data is handled with consent and stored securely, in line with ethical research practice.
